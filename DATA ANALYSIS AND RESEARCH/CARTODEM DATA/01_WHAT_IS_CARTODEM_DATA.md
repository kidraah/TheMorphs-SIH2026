# What is CartoDEM Data?

## Definition
CartoDEM (Cartosat Digital Elevation Model) is a national, high-resolution Digital Elevation Model (DEM) for the Indian subcontinent. 

## Core Identity
It is a 3D topographic map of the Earth's bare surface (specifically, it is a Digital Surface Model, DSM, often processed into a DEM). It completely strips away the visual "photo" of the ground (trees, houses, roads) and replaces it with pure geometry. It tells you exactly how high the ground is above sea level at any given point in India.
