# Source and Acquisition of CartoDEM

## 1. The Satellite: Cartosat-1
Cartosat-1 (launched by ISRO in 2005) was a dedicated stereoscopic earth observation satellite. Unlike standard satellites that take a single flat picture, Cartosat-1 carried two cameras:
- **Camera A (Fore):** Pointed 26° forward.
- **Camera B (Aft):** Pointed -5° backward.
As the satellite flew over India, it took two pictures of the exact same mountain a few seconds apart, from two different angles.

## 2. Processing by NRSC
The National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC) in Hyderabad takes these millions of stereo pairs and processes them. They apply Ground Control Points (GCPs) measured by surveyors on the ground to ensure the space data matches the real world.

## 3. How We Access It
The 30m resolution version (Version 3) is entirely free for Indian citizens. It is downloaded in 1° x 1° tiles from the **Bhuvan Geoportal (NIDHI platform)** maintained by ISRO.
