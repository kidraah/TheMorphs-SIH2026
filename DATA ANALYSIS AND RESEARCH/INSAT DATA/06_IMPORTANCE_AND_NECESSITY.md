# Importance and Necessity of INSAT Data

## 1. The Temporal Advantage (15-Minute Rule)
A cloudburst can form, dump its rain, and dissipate in under 90 minutes. 
Traditional rain gauges are checked once every 24 hours. Polar satellites pass over only twice a day. By the time they see the storm, the flash flood has already destroyed the village.
INSAT, staring constantly and providing data every 15 to 30 minutes, is the **ONLY** observational tool capable of catching the explosive growth phase of a cloudburst in real-time.

## 2. The Himalayan Radar Blindspot
Why not use Doppler Weather Radars (DWR)? Radars are ground-based and shoot beams horizontally. In the Himalayas, the radar beam hits the first mountain peak and is blocked, leaving vast valleys behind it completely blind (beam blockage).
INSAT looks straight down from space. Mountains cannot block its view. It provides 100% coverage of the most complex, inaccessible terrain on Earth.
