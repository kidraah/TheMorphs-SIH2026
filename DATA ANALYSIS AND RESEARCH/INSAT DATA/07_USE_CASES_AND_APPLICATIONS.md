# Use Cases and Applications of INSAT Data

## 1. AI-Driven Nowcasting (SIH26077)
In our Early Warning System, INSAT Cloud Top Temperature (CTT) drop rates are the ultimate real-time trigger. If the AI detects CTT cooling by >10K in 30 minutes, it flags extreme updraft development. The HEM QPE data serves as the target variable for training the AI to predict localized rainfall intensity.

## 2. Cyclone Tracking and Intensity Estimation
INSAT is critical for tracking Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea cyclones. Using the Dvorak technique (analyzing the eye and cloud band temperatures), meteorologists estimate the cyclone's wind speed and trajectory purely from INSAT imagery.

## 3. Aviation Safety (Volcanic Ash and Fog)
Using the difference between two thermal channels (Split Window technique), INSAT can differentiate between water clouds and volcanic ash/sandstorms, protecting commercial aircraft. Nighttime microphysics products highlight dense fog over North India during winter, crucial for flight and train scheduling.

## 4. Agricultural Drought Monitoring
By calculating the NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) from the visible and near-infrared channels, the government tracks crop health and drought onset at a national scale.
