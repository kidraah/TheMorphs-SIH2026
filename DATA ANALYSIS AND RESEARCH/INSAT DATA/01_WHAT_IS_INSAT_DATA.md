# What is INSAT Data?

## Definition
INSAT (Indian National Satellite System) data is meteorological and environmental observational data captured from space. Specifically for weather, the data comes from **INSAT-3D** and **INSAT-3DR**.

## Core Identity
It is a **Geostationary** observation dataset. Unlike polar-orbiting satellites that fly close to earth and only pass over India twice a day, INSAT satellites sit 35,786 km above the equator, orbiting at the exact speed of Earth's rotation. This means they stare constantly at the exact same spot (the Indian subcontinent) 24/7.

It provides raw "snapshots" of the atmosphere across different wavelengths of light (visible, infrared, water vapor) every 15 to 30 minutes.
