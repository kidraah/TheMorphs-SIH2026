# What Does IMDAA Show?

IMDAA provides a 4-Dimensional (Lat, Lon, Altitude, Time) representation of the atmosphere. 

## 1. The Grid
- **Horizontal:** The entire Indian subcontinent and surrounding oceans divided into a grid of squares, each ~12 km x 12 km.
- **Vertical:** The atmosphere divided into 37 vertical layers (pressure levels) from the surface (1000 hPa) up to the stratosphere (1 hPa).
- **Temporal:** A snapshot of this entire 3D grid every 1 hour (for surface) and 6 hours (for upper atmosphere).

## 2. The Variables (What it actually measures)
At every single point in that 3D grid, IMDAA shows:
- **Kinematics:** Wind speed and direction (U and V vectors).
- **Thermodynamics:** Air Temperature, Pressure, and Geopotential Height.
- **Moisture:** Specific Humidity, Relative Humidity.
- **Surface Fluxes:** Soil moisture, surface runoff, sensible heat flux, and latent heat flux (how much heat/moisture the ground is releasing).
