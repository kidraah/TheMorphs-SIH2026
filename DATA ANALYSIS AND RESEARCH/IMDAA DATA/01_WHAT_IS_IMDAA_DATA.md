# What is IMDAA Data?

## Definition
IMDAA (Indian Monsoon Data Assimilation and Analysis) is a high-resolution regional atmospheric **reanalysis** dataset. 

## What is "Reanalysis"?
Reanalysis is a scientific method that combines past observations (from satellites, weather balloons, ships, and ground stations) with a modern numerical weather prediction (NWP) model. It "re-analyzes" historical weather using today's advanced physics equations. 

## Core Identity
Unlike a pure forecast (which predicts the future) or pure observation (which only exists where a sensor is located), IMDAA provides a continuous, gap-free, 3D grid of the atmosphere over the Indian subcontinent from 1979 to 2020. It acts as the closest possible approximation to the "true" historical state of the atmosphere.
