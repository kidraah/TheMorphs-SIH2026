# Use Cases and Applications of IMDAA

## 1. Early Warning Systems (SIH26077)
In our specific use case, IMDAA is used to extract thermodynamic precursors (CAPE, CIN, Moisture) that occur *before* a cloudburst. By analyzing past disasters in the IMDAA archive, we train AI to recognize the invisible atmospheric setups that lead to flash floods.

## 2. Climate Change Attribution
Scientists use IMDAA's 40-year history to track long-term trends. Are monsoons becoming more erratic? Is the average temperature of the Tibetan plateau rising? IMDAA provides the continuous historical baseline to prove these trends.

## 3. Renewable Energy Forecasting
Wind and solar companies use IMDAA historical wind and cloud-cover profiles to determine the most profitable locations to build new wind farms or solar parks in India.

## 4. Agricultural Planning
Tracking soil moisture anomalies and long-term rainfall deficits helps the government declare drought zones and plan crop cycles (Kharif/Rabi seasons).
